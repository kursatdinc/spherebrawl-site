Sphere Brawl

Same seed. Same fight.

Sphere Brawl makes short videos of weapon-ball battles. Every fight is simulated by a deterministic engine, so the same seed and the same two classes always replay the exact same fight.

The videos are published on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok by a small tool that belongs to the channel owner and uploads only to the channel owner's own accounts.
