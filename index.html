Sphere Brawl

Two spheres. One winner.

Sphere Brawl is a channel of short weapon-ball battles. Two spheres, each with its own weapon, fight until one is left standing.

New videos go up on YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Facebook. The channel owner uploads them with a small publishing tool of their own, only to their own accounts.
